# 2. Project 01: CPSE–MSME Receivables Intelligence Agent

## 2.1 Problem & Industry Context
MSMEs in India supplying Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs such as NTPC, BHEL, ONGC, SAIL, IOCL) and Indian Railways suffer from severe working capital traps:
* **The Verification Friction:** Invoices undergo protracted physical and multi-portal verification across stores, quality inspectors, consignees, and accounts departments.
* **TReDS Acceptance Bottleneck:** Even though the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) mandates CPSEs to settle invoices via TReDS platforms (RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart), invoices sit unapproved due to minor clerical mismatches.
* **Opaque Cash Cycles:** Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) often stretches from the legally mandated 45 days to **120–180+ days**, driving MSMEs into high-cost unorganized debt or bank cash credit limits ($12\text{--}16\%$ APR).

## 2.2 Regulatory Catalysts (2026 Environment)
1. **MSMED Act 2006 (Section 15 & 16):** Mandates settlement within 45 days. Delayed payments attract **compound interest with monthly rests at 3x the RBI Bank Rate** ($\approx 19.5\%$ annual compound rate).
2. **Income Tax Act (Section 43B(h)):** Disallows tax deductions on purchases from MSMEs if payments remain unpaid past the 45-day statutory limit.

## 2.3 System Architecture & Technical Implementation
* **Pre-Submission 4-Way Matcher:** Reconciles PO Number, line items, rates, delivery challan receipt quantities, and tax codes before dispatch.
* **Statutory Compound Interest Engine:** Real-time calculation of compound interest under Section 16:
  $$\text{Total Dues} = P \times \left(1 + \frac{r_{\text{stat}}}{12}\right)^{n_{\text{months}}}$$
* **TReDS vs. Bank Cash Credit Optimizer:** Computes the net liquidity delta between TReDS auction bids and bank OD rates.
* **Autonomous Escalation Agent:** Auto-generates statutory demand notices, MSME Samadhaan facilitation filings, and executive settlement memos.

## 2.4 Financials, Pricing & Unit Economics
* **Target Audience:** ~65,000 active MSMEs registered on GeM and CPSE vendor directories with annual turnover of ₹10 Cr – ₹250 Cr.
* **Pricing Model:** ₹5,000 – ₹25,000 / month / vendor SaaS subscription.
* **Customer ROI Proof:** For an MSME with ₹20 Cr CPSE receivables book, reducing DSO by 20 days saves **₹6.5 Lakhs in annual interest**, easily covering the ₹1.2 Lakh annual SaaS fee ($5.4\times$ ROI).

# 3. Project 02: Construction Material Intelligence (CMI) — Recommended Proposal

## 3.1 Problem & Industrial Context
India produces over **450 Million Tonnes** of cement annually. Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) producers and infrastructure EPCs operate in a low-margin, high-risk environment governed by a **28-day quality feedback blindspot**:
* **The 28-Day Lag:** Standard structural compliance requires 28-day water-cured cube compressive strength tests. If a batch fails, the structural slab or bridge pier has already been cast and loaded.
* **The Over-Design Tax:** Because raw aggregate moisture, sand gradation, and cement reactivity fluctuate daily, batching plant managers deliberately add **15–30 kg of excess cement per m³** as an insurance buffer.
* **Fragmented Data:** Batching PLC logs, aggregate moisture tests, and lab cube crush registers live in disconnected silos.

## 3.2 Technical Innovation: Physics-Informed ML (Cold-Start Solution)
Pure machine learning fails in commercial concrete because a new plant may only possess 30–50 historical lab tests. CMI resolves this using a **Domain-Constrained Physics + ML Hybrid**:

# 4. Project 03: Civil Engineering Evidence & Claims Intelligence

## 4.1 Problem & Industry Context
Major infrastructure projects (metros, expressways, bridges, railways) executed by EPC contractors (L&T, Afcons, Tata Projects, NCC) experience massive revenue leakage:
* **Unbilled Variations & Disputed Extra Items:** Site conditions frequently deviate from initial tender drawings (e.g., deeper rock strata during piling). Contractors execute the work, but due to missing or disconnected records across drawings, photos, and measurement books, **3–7% of project contract value is lost or delayed for years in arbitration**.
* **Audit Certification Roadblocks:** Government employers and PMCs reject Running Account (RA) bills whenever cumulative execution exceeds contractual variation limits (e.g., CPWD / FIDIC Clause 12 $>10\%$ warning, $>25\%$ rate revision threshold).

## 4.2 Technical Architecture
* **Engineering Evidence Graph:** Connects heterogeneous engineering data into a structured knowledge graph:
  $$\text{Tender BOQ Item} \longleftrightarrow \text{Revised AutoCAD DWG} \longleftrightarrow \text{Certified MB Log} \longleftrightarrow \text{Geo-Tagged Photo}$$
* **Contractual Deviation Monitor:** Continuously audits executed quantities against tender limits, alerting project managers before exceeding the 25% statutory ceiling.
* **Audit-Proof Claim Dossier Generator:** Automatically compiles tamper-evident dossiers citing tender clauses, verified level sheets, and photographic rock core logs.

## 4.3 Financials & Unit Economics
* **Target Audience:** Tier-1/2 Civil EPC Contractors executing projects of ₹100 Cr – ₹2,000 Cr value.
* **Pricing:** ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000 / month / project package.
* **Customer ROI:** On a ₹200 Cr project, recovering just **one disputed ₹50 Lakhs variation claim** pays for the software for the entire 3-year construction duration ($10\times\text{--}25\times\text{ ROI}$).

# 5. Project 04: WaterResilience AI

## 5.1 Problem & Global Context
Metropolises worldwide face severe urban flash flooding due to intense cloudbursts and rapid paving:
* **The 4-Hour Simulation Bottleneck:** Standard 1D/2D hydrodynamic numerical solvers (EPA-SWMM, MIKE Urban, TUFLOW) require **3 to 8 hours** to compute a single high-resolution storm event on a $50\text{ km}^2$ city mesh.
* **Inability to Explore Options:** Civil drainage consultants designing municipal master plans can only manually evaluate 5–10 design scenarios within their consulting budgets.

## 5.2 Technical Innovation: Physics-Informed ML Surrogate
* **10,000x Computational Speedup:** Approximates 1D/2D Saint-Venant hydraulic and Manning pipe flow equations in **under 50 milliseconds**.
* **Massive Monte Carlo Simulation:** Evaluates 10,000+ extreme storm permutations, climate change scenarios, and pipe clogging permutations instantly.
* **Sponge City (SUDS) ROI Optimizer:** Computes cost-effectiveness ($\text{INR Lakhs per }\text{m}^3/\text{s}\text{ mitigated}$) comparing Blue-Green interventions (retention ponds, bioswales) vs. heavy Gray infrastructure (box culverts).

## 5.3 Go-To-Market & Commercial Strategy
* **Beachhead Wedge:** Sell directly to **Infrastructure Design Consultancies** (AECOM, Jacobs, WSP, Tata Consulting Engineers, L&T Infra Engineering) who prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPRs) for government bodies.
* **Pricing:** ₹75,000 – ₹1,50,000 / month / firm (SaaS) or ₹25 Lakhs – ₹75 Lakhs / city / year for Smart City Integrated Command and Control Centers (ICCC).
