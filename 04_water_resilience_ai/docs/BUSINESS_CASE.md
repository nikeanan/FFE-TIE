# Business Case: WaterResilience AI

## 1. Opportunity Overview
Municipal corporations, smart cities, and infrastructure design consultancies struggle to model urban flood risk rapidly. Traditional hydrodynamic modeling tools (EPA-SWMM, MIKE Urban, TUFLOW) require hours to simulate a single storm, making real-time disaster management and iterative master drainage planning impractical.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
* **Physics-Informed ML Surrogate:** Approximates 1D/2D Saint-Venant hydraulic equations, reducing simulation time from 4+ hours to under 50 milliseconds (10,000x speedup).
* **Multi-Scenario Monte Carlo Modeling:** Enables instant testing of thousands of extreme rainfall permutations and climate resilience scenarios.
* **Sponge City & SUDS Intervention Optimizer:** Compares Blue-Green infrastructure (detention ponds, bioswales, rain gardens) vs. Gray infrastructure (box culverts) on cost-per-cubic-meter-mitigated.

## 3. Go-to-Market Strategy & Pricing
* **Beachhead Wedge:** Sell to Infrastructure Design Consultancies (AECOM, Jacobs, WSP, Tata Consulting Engineers) preparing Master Drainage DPRs.
* **Pricing:** ₹75,000 – ₹1,50,000 / month / firm (SaaS) or ₹25 Lakhs – ₹75 Lakhs / city / year (Smart City ICCC platform).
