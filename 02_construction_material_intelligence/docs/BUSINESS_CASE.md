# Business Case: Construction Material Intelligence (CMI)

## 1. Opportunity Overview
Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) producers and infrastructure contractors face low operating margins and high raw material variability. To avoid the risk of 28-day cube test failures, plants artificially over-design concrete mixes with 15–30 kg of excess cement per m³.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
* **Predictive Strength at Batching ($t=0$):** Physics-informed ML model predicts 7-day and 28-day compressive strength immediately when the truck is batched.
* **Real-Time QC Alerts:** Detects batching scale drift and aggregate moisture spikes (e.g., post-rain sand moisture shifts) before concrete is dispatched.
* **Mix Optimization:** Safely reduces cement content by 10–15 kg/m³ while staying strictly compliant with IS 10262:2019 and IS 456:2000 durability standards.

## 3. Financial ROI
* For a plant producing 7,500 m³/month: Saving 12 kg cement/m³ saves 1,800 bags/month = **₹6.48 Lakhs/month**.
* Software subscription: **₹20,000/month/plant** -> **>30x ROI**.

## 4. Strategic Defensibility
* **Plant-Specific Quarry Moat:** Accumulation of historical batch logs, regional aggregate absorption factors, and cement brand reactivities creates high switching costs.
* **Expansion Path:** Concrete $\rightarrow$ Aggregates $\rightarrow$ Asphalt $\rightarrow$ Rebar.
