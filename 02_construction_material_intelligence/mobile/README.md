# 📱 CMI Mobile App (Android & iOS)

> **Construction Material Intelligence Mobile Plant Twin & Field QA Inspector**  
> Built with React Native / Expo for concrete batching plant managers, field testing engineers, and EPC quality auditors.

## Features
- **Mix Design & IS 456 Checker**: Real-time water-binder & minimum cementitious verification.
- **SCADA Scale Drift & Moisture QC**: Direct live sensor telemetry & free water calculations.
- **Embodied Carbon Calculator**: Scope 3 CO₂e/m³ tracking with GRIHA & IGBC green rating tiers.
- **Digital Quality Certificates**: Instant export of signed tamper-evident batch certificates.

## Build Standalone:
- Android APK: `npx eas-cli build -p android --profile preview`
- iOS IPA: `npx eas-cli build -p ios --profile preview`
