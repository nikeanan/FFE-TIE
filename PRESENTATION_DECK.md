## Slide 3: Spotlight on Recommended Proposal — Construction Material Intelligence (CMI)
* **The Problem:** RMC plants artificially over-design concrete by 15–30 kg cement/m³ to avoid the 28-day cube test failure blindspot.
* **The Solution:** Hybrid Physics (Abram's Law + IS 10262:2019) + Bayesian ML model predicting compressive strength at batching ($t=0$).
* **Direct Value:** Saves 12 kg cement/m³ = **₹6.48 Lakhs/month saved per plant** against a **₹20,000/month SaaS cost**.

---

## Slide 4: Technology Architecture & Cold-Start Solution
* **Cold-Start Innovation:** Pure ML requires 10,000+ rows; CMI uses **domain physics as a strong prior**, needing only 15–20 historical batches to calibrate.
* **Real-Time Edge Alerts:** Monitors aggregate moisture spikes and scale drift in real time per IS 4926 standards.
* **Continuous Learning:** As cube crush results are logged over months, plant-specific quarry calibration becomes an unassailable switching moat.

## Slide 5: 12-Month Execution Roadmap
* **Months 1–3:** Pilot validation across 3 RMC plants in Delhi-NCR / Mumbai.
* **Months 4–6:** Automated batcher PLC edge connectors (Schwing Stetter, Command Alkon) & mobile alert dashboard.
* **Months 7–9:** Commercial SaaS rollout to 25 commercial RMC plants.
* **Months 10–12:** Expansion into Low-Carbon Concrete (fly ash/GGBS replacement) and Asphalt/Bitumen quality intelligence.
