# Business Case: Civil Engineering Evidence & Claims Intelligence

## 1. Opportunity Overview
Infrastructure EPC contractors executing large highway, metro, bridge, and industrial projects lose 3–7% of total contract value in unbilled variations, disputed extra items, and prolonged arbitration due to fragmented field records.

## 2. Core Value Proposition
* **Multi-Modal Evidence Graph:** Connects Tender BOQ items $\leftrightarrow$ Revised Drawings $\leftrightarrow$ Measurement Book (MB) pages $\leftrightarrow$ Site Inspection Photos $\leftrightarrow$ Formal Correspondence.
* **Proactive Variation Safeguard:** Tracks cumulative executed quantities against CPWD / FIDIC Clause 12 deviation thresholds (>10% warning, >25% rate analysis trigger).
* **Audit-Proof Claim Dossiers:** Assembles tamper-evident substantiation dossiers citing contract clauses, certified measurement logs, and photographic evidence.

## 3. Financial ROI & Pricing
* On a ₹200 Cr project, variations account for ~₹10 Cr. Preventing a single disputed variation deduction of ₹50 Lakhs pays for software across the entire project lifecycle.
* **Pricing:** ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000 / month / project package.
