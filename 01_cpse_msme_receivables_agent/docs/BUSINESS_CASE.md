# Business Case: CPSE–MSME Receivables Intelligence Agent

## 1. Opportunity Overview
MSMEs supplying Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) and large PSUs face severe working capital bottlenecks due to documentation mismatches, fragmented multi-tier approvals, and delayed acceptance. 

## 2. 2026 Regulatory & Financial Catalysts
* **MSMED Act Section 15 & 16:** Mandates payment within 45 days, imposing penal compound interest at 3x the RBI Bank Rate for delayed payments.
* **Income Tax Act Section 43B(h):** Disallows tax deductions on purchases from MSMEs unless paid within statutory deadlines.
* **TReDS Mandatory CPSE Integration:** CPSEs are under strict regulatory directives to onboard and settle MSME invoices via RBI-authorized TReDS platforms (RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart).

## 3. Product Features & Wedge
1. **Pre-Submission 4-Way Audit:** Auto-reconciles PO line items, Delivery Challans, e-Way bills, and Tax Invoices to catch rate/quantity/tax mismatches before submission.
2. **Statutory Aging & Interest Engine:** Real-time calculation of claimable Section 16 interest and Section 43B(h) disallowance exposure.
3. **Autonomous Dispute & Escalation Drafter:** Pre-drafts formal demand notices, reconciliations, and MSEFC facilitation council filing packages.
4. **TReDS Financing Optimizer:** Compares real-time discounting bids on TReDS against the MSME's existing bank cash-credit/OD borrowing rates to maximize net liquidity.

## 4. Monetization & Unit Economics
* **Target:** Tier-2/3 MSMEs with ₹10 Cr – ₹200 Cr annual revenue supplying defense, railways, power, and oil & gas PSUs.
* **Pricing:** ₹5,000 – ₹25,000 / month SaaS subscription + 0.05% – 0.10% optional success fee on accelerated TReDS settlement.
* **Customer ROI:** Reducing DSO by 15 days on a ₹20 Cr annual book saves ₹3–6 Lakhs in annual interest expense.
